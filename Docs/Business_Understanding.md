# Business Understanding

## Project Overview

This project analyzes sales data from a fictional global retail company operating across multiple countries and regions. The company sells products in three main categories: Furniture, Office Supplies, and Technology. The dataset contains 51,291 records and 24 attributes describing customer orders, product information, sales performance, shipping details, and geographical locations.

The primary objective of this project is to transform raw transactional data into meaningful business insights that support strategic decision-making. The analysis will follow a complete data analytics workflow, including data understanding, cleaning, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis (EDA), dashboard development, and business reporting.

---

## Business Objectives

The main business objectives of this project are:

* Increase overall sales revenue.
* Maximize business profitability.
* Evaluate the impact of discounts on profit.
* Identify high-performing and underperforming products.
* Analyze sales performance across countries, regions, and cities.
* Understand customer purchasing behavior.
* Improve shipping and delivery performance.
* Support data-driven business decisions through interactive dashboards.

---

## Business Questions

The analysis aims to answer questions such as:

* Which product categories generate the highest sales?
* Which categories generate the highest profit?
* Which products are the most profitable?
* Which products consistently generate losses?
* Which regions achieve the highest sales?
* Which regions generate the highest profit?
* Are high discounts associated with lower profitability?
* Which customers contribute the highest revenue?
* Which customer segments are the most valuable?
* How have sales and profits changed over time?
* Which shipping modes are used most frequently?
* Are there geographical areas with consistently poor performance?
* Which cities should receive more business attention?
* What factors appear to influence profitability?

---

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project will monitor several business KPIs, including:

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin
* Number of Orders
* Average Order Value (AOV)
* Average Discount
* Total Quantity Sold
* Sales by Category
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Region
* Sales Growth Over Time
* Top 10 Products by Sales
* Top 10 Customers by Revenue

---

## Project Scope

This project focuses on descriptive and diagnostic analytics using historical sales data. The scope includes data cleaning, data validation, SQL analysis, exploratory data analysis, KPI development, business reporting, and Power BI dashboard creation.

Predictive modeling and machine learning are intentionally excluded because the available dataset is designed primarily for business intelligence and historical performance analysis rather than forecasting or predictive tasks.
